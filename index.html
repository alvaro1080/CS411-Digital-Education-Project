MISSION 03 · TIME FOR TELLING

You just observed two different growth rates.

Here is the theory behind what you just saw.

B
ALGORITHM A

Bubble Sort

Repeatedly compares neighboring elements. In the worst case, many comparisons and swaps are required.

O(n²)
M
ALGORITHM B

Merge Sort

Divides the list into smaller parts and merges them efficiently. Each level processes the elements once.

O(n log n)
!
THE KEY IDEA

Different algorithms can scale very differently.

Bubble Sort can require roughly quadratic work, while Merge Sort grows much more slowly as the input becomes larger.
